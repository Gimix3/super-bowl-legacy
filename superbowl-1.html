The Beginning of a Legacy

The first Super Bowl was played on January 15, 1967, bringing together the champions of the NFL and the AFL for the first time.

The Green Bay Packers, led by legendary head coach Vince Lombardi, defeated the Kansas City Chiefs 35–10 in Los Angeles.

Bart Starr was named the game's first Super Bowl MVP, while the Packers began what would become one of the greatest legacies in NFL history.
