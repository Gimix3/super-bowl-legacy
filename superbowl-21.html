GAME HIGHLIGHTS

THE GIANTS' FIRST SUPER BOWL TITLE

New York defeated Denver 39–20 to claim the first Super Bowl championship in franchise history.

MVP — PHIL SIMMS

Giants quarterback Phil Simms was named Super Bowl XXI MVP after completing 22 of 25 passes for 268 yards and three touchdowns.

A MEMORABLE PERFORMANCE

The Giants scored 30 unanswered points after Denver briefly took the lead in the third quarter.
